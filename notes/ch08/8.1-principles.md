## 關鍵點

### 為什麼需要特權操作

> "In the basic integer core described so far, **a program can access any feature and any location
>  in memory. This raises two problems: a faulty program can crash the machine, and a malicious
>  program can compromise the machine. Processors add privileged modes to provide protection and
>  security to prevent these problems.**"
> — p.335

中文解釋：**兩個問題，對應 8.1.1 節的兩個詞：**
- **有 bug 的程式會讓機器當掉** → 需要 **protection（保護）**
- **惡意程式會危害機器** → 需要 **security（安全）**

**第二個動機（p.335）：**
> "Processors also must be able to respond to unusual program events called *exceptions* or *faults*
>  (e.g., trying to access nonexistent memory) and external events called *interrupts* (e.g., a
>  keystroke or periodic timer). **The processor responds with a *trap* that saves the current
>  program counter (PC), raises the privilege mode if necessary, and jumps to a *trap handler* that
>  is located at a specified memory address. The trap handler may save other state, respond to the
>  trap, restore the saved state, and restore the PC to continue the program.**"

（呼應 2.3.5 節。**trap handler 也叫 exception handler、interrupt handler，或簡稱 handler**，
p.335 邊欄。）

### 安全 vs 保護：兩個相關但不同的詞

> "Security and protection are closely related but distinct terms. In this chapter, we define
>  **security as the general problem of preventing unauthorized agents from accessing a computer
>  system and detecting and mitigating damage if access does occur. Protection refers to specific
>  hardware and software mechanisms to prevent both malicious and accidental access to resources.**"
> — p.336

中文解釋：**區分很清楚：**

| 詞 | 範圍 |
| --- | --- |
| **Security（安全）** | **廣義問題**：防止未授權存取，以及偵測和減輕已發生的損害 |
| **Protection（保護）** | **具體機制**：防止惡意和意外存取資源的硬體與軟體手段 |

**「安全」是目標，「保護」是手段。本章講的是保護。**

### 歷史教訓：硬體先行，軟體落後二十年

> "Early computers only ran one program at a time, but developers soon discovered a need to share
>  the computer among multiple programs and users. **The first general-purpose time sharing was the
>  Compatible Time Sharing System (CTSS) running on a modified IBM 7094** [Corbato62]. Designers
>  immediately discovered the need to protect one program from another and added architectural
>  features to address this need."
> — p.336

> "**Intel introduced memory management and protection in its 80286 processor in 1982, but developers
>  of operating systems were slow to extend their code to take advantage of these features because of
>  the challenge of maintaining backward compatibility. For example, Microsoft operating systems such
>  as Windows 95 were notoriously unstable because a bug in one program could bring down the whole
>  system. Almost twenty years after the hardware support for protection on PCs became available,
>  Microsoft Windows 2000 took advantage of it to give mainstream users a moderately stable computing
>  experience.**"
> — p.336

中文解釋：**這段歷史很有教育意義：硬體 1982 年就有了，軟體要到 2000 年才用上——差了 18 年。**

**原因是「維持向後相容的挑戰」**（呼應 1.5 節 x86 的相容性包袱、
2.6 節 MIPS delay slot 的教訓）。

**Windows 95 之所以惡名昭彰地不穩定，是因為它沒用硬體的保護機制**——
一個程式的 bug 可以拖垮整個系統。

**教訓：好的硬體機制，如果軟體不用，就等於沒有。**

### 現代的資安威脅

> "**Malware has become an enormous economic drain and national security threat.** Cybercriminals and
>  governments use malware to steal personal information, hold corporate data ransom, and attack
>  critical infrastructure such as power plants and military labs. **Around 2010, a computer worm
>  called Stuxnet, attributed to Israel and the United States, reportedly wiped out 20% of Iran's
>  nuclear centrifuges by causing them to spin out of control.... In 2015 and 2016, Russian hackers
>  repeatedly attacked Ukraine's power grid and shut off power in the capital city Kyiv.
>  The FBI recorded $16B of cybercrime losses reported by US businesses and individuals in 2024
>  [IC3-21]; this is likely just the tip of the iceberg.**"
> — p.336

中文解釋：**三個具體案例和一個數字：**

| 事件 | 影響 |
| --- | --- |
| **Stuxnet（約 2010）** | **摧毀伊朗 20% 的核離心機** |
| **俄羅斯駭客（2015、2016）** | **關閉基輔的電力** |
| **中國駭客（2024，NSA 公布）** | 侵入美國能源、交通、通訊、水處理等關鍵基礎設施至少五年 |
| **FBI 記錄（2024）** | **160 億美元的網路犯罪損失**（且只是冰山一角） |

（呼應 1.8 節的地緣政治議題。）

**引言（p.336 邊欄，Cisco CEO John Chambers）：**
> "**There are only two types of companies: those that have been hacked and those that don't know
>  they have been hacked.**"

### 三種攻擊類型

> "Malware threats include confidentiality, integrity, and availability attacks. **A *confidentiality*
>  attack steals sensitive information such as credit card numbers. An *integrity* attack tampers with
>  data, such as changing grades on a transcript or encrypting a hospital's patient records until a
>  ransom is paid. An *availability* attack denies service, such as bringing down a website.**"
> — p.337

中文解釋：**這就是資安的 CIA 三要素：**

| 攻擊類型 | 目標 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confidentiality（機密性）** | **竊取** | 偷信用卡號 |
| **Integrity（完整性）** | **竄改** | 改成績、勒索軟體加密病歷 |
| **Availability（可用性）** | **阻斷服務** | 讓網站下線 |

### 攻擊的現實與防禦

> "**Any device connected to the internet is now under nearly constant attack by automated scripts
>  probing for access. Most attacks target known vulnerabilities, such as weak passwords, bugs in
>  outdated programs, or gullible employees.** Rigorous information technology (IT) policies of using
>  unique secure passwords, deploying firewalls and antivirus software, quickly patching security
>  holes, and training employees about suspicious messages can mitigate most of these attacks.
>  **The most sophisticated attackers, often state sponsored, also seek out new bugs and
>  vulnerabilities that are unknown to vendors and users. Such *zero-day* exploits are used to take
>  control of phones, computers, and critical infrastructure for spying and economic warfare.**"
> — p.337

中文解釋：**兩類攻擊者，防禦方式不同：**

| 攻擊者 | 手法 | 防禦 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **自動化腳本（多數）** | **已知弱點**：弱密碼、過期程式的 bug、易上當的員工 | **IT 政策**：強密碼、防火牆、快速修補、員工訓練 |
| **國家級攻擊者** | **zero-day**（廠商和使用者都不知道的新漏洞） | 難防 |

**「Zombie armies（殭屍大軍）」用來探測其他電腦或發動分散式阻斷服務攻擊**（p.337）。

### 特權模式：把攻擊面縮小

> "**Securing a large amount of code, especially code from untrusted sources, is very difficult.
>  Larger code bases have more bugs that can be exploited. A first step is to divide a system into at
>  least two *privilege modes*.** Most processes operate in user mode (U-mode). **U-mode programs can
>  only access resources such as memory and files granted to them by the OS kernel. They are protected
>  from accessing other resources, so even if a U-mode program is malicious or buggy, it cannot read
>  or corrupt resources owned by another program or the OS. When a process needs to request more
>  resources, it must call the OS, which runs in supervisor mode (S-mode). The OS verifies that the
>  request is valid and assigns the resources, then returns to the U-mode portion of the program.
>  Now, the attack surface is limited to the smaller OS kernel rather than the entire set of programs
>  on the machine.**"
> — p.337

中文解釋：**核心論證，一句話：**

**「攻擊面從『機器上所有程式』縮小到『較小的 OS 核心』。」**

**推理鏈：**
1. **程式碼越多，可被利用的 bug 越多**
2. 把系統分成兩種特權模式
3. **U-mode 程式只能存取 OS 核心授予的資源**
4. 所以即使 U-mode 程式惡意或有 bug，**也動不了別的程式或 OS 的資源**
5. 要更多資源就必須呼叫 OS（S-mode），**OS 驗證請求是否合法**
6. **→ 只要保護好 OS 核心就好**

（呼應 2.3.1 節。）

### Trap 的角色

> "Recall that a processor takes a trap to stop normal program execution and jump to a trap handler
>  when an interrupt or exceptional program event occurs. **Traps are an important protection
>  mechanism, providing a secure method to invoke system calls at a higher privilege level and a way
>  to stop programs that attempt to access unauthorized data or code.**"
> — p.337

中文解釋：**trap 的兩個保護功能：**
1. **提供安全的方式在更高特權等級呼叫系統服務**（`ecall`）
2. **阻止試圖存取未授權資料或程式碼的程式**（fault）

**注意這兩個方向相反：一個是「讓你上去」，一個是「不讓你亂來」。
但用的是同一套機制。**

### Precise vs Imprecise Trap（本節最重要的技術概念）

> "**A precise trap is one associated with a specific instruction such that all previous instructions
>  have retired (completed), and no later instructions have modified architectural state or memory.
>  In contrast, an imprecise trap is one in which the pipeline cannot be restarted at a specific
>  instruction, for example, if some instructions have retired out of order when the trap occurs.**"
> — p.338

中文解釋：**Precise trap 的定義有兩個條件，缺一不可：**
1. **所有更早的指令都已經退休（完成）**
2. **所有更晚的指令都還沒改動架構狀態或記憶體**

**換句話說：trap 發生的那一刻，機器的狀態剛好停在「執行到某條指令之前」的乾淨點。**

**（p.338 邊欄：「RISC-V 只支援 precise trap。」）**

**書上的反例非常清楚（p.338），一定要跟一遍：**
```asm
0x1000: div t1, t2, t3
0x1004: add s0, s0, s1
```
> "Suppose the div instruction takes 20 cycles, and the independent add instruction takes 1 cycle.
>  **Suppose the core allows the add to write its result to the register file before the div retires.
>  Then suppose a timer interrupt occurs after the add but before the div retires, causing an
>  imprecise trap. After the trap handler completes, to what PC should it return?**
>  **If it returns to 0x1000, the div will reissue properly, but add will execute a second time and
>  produce an incorrect result in s0. If it returns to x01008, the div instruction will not have
>  executed at all, leaving an incorrect result in t1.**"

**兩難的處境：**

| 回到哪 | 後果 |
| --- | --- |
| **0x1000** | div 正確重跑，**但 add 會執行兩次** → s0 錯 |
| **0x1008** | **div 根本沒執行過** → t1 錯 |

**沒有正確答案——這就是 imprecise trap 的問題。**

### Precise trap 對微架構的要求

> "**Precise traps allow a program to resume from a well-defined place. To precisely handle traps, a
>  pipeline should have a retire or commit stage at the end of the pipeline to delay modifying
>  architectural state until all previous instructions have retired. For in-order processors, the
>  writeback stage at the end of the pipeline serves this role. Out-of-order processors are more
>  complicated and may use a reorder buffer to delay committing results to architectural state until
>  all previous instructions have completed.**"
> — p.338

中文解釋：**這段解釋了 7.2 節那個設計原則的理由：**

| 處理器類型 | 怎麼實現 precise trap |
| --- | --- |
| **循序（in-order）** | **pipeline 末端的 Writeback 級就是 commit 級** |
| **亂序（out-of-order）** | **需要 reorder buffer**，延遲提交直到所有更早的指令完成 |

**呼應 7.2 節邊欄：「所有架構狀態的改變都等到 Writeback 級才提交。」
——現在知道為什麼了：這是 precise trap 的必要條件。**

**兩個實際需要 precise trap 的場景（p.338）：**
> "**For example, if a load causes a virtual memory page fault, the trap handler should fetch the
>  missing page and then resume the load without having other partially completed instructions
>  leaving the system in an inconsistent state. Similarly, if a debugger takes a trap when reaching a
>  breakpoint, all the instructions before the breakpoint should be complete, and none of the
>  instructions after the breakpoint should appear to have been started.**"

| 場景 | 為什麼需要 precise |
| --- | --- |
| **虛擬記憶體 page fault** | **handler 載入缺失的頁後要能重跑那條 load** |
| **除錯器中斷點** | **斷點前的指令都要完成，斷點後的都不能開始** |

**結論（p.338）：**
> "**Handling *imprecise* traps in a way other than terminating the program is complicated and
>  sometimes impossible, so most processors generate precise traps.**"

**「除了直接終止程式以外，處理 imprecise trap 很複雜，有時根本不可能。」**

### CSR 的三類用途

> "Most processors have CSRs that report or change the behavior of the processor. Examples of CSRs are:
>  - **Registers to control traps, such as enabling/disabling interrupts or saving the program counter**
>  - **Registers to enable/disable optional features**
>  - **Performance counters to assist profiling programs**"
> — p.338

中文解釋：**三類，對應本章後面的三大主題：**

| 類別 | 例子 | 在哪一節 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **控制 trap** | 啟用/停用中斷、儲存 PC | 8.2.3.1（`mstatus`）、8.2.5 |
| **啟用/停用選配功能** | `misa`、`menvcfg` | 8.2.3.2、8.2.3.6 |
| **效能計數器** | `mcycle`、`minstret` | 8.2.3.4 |

## 所以呢

**這節建立了特權操作的動機和一個關鍵技術概念。**

三件最該帶走的事：

1. **特權模式的核心價值是「縮小攻擊面」**——
   從「機器上所有程式」縮小到「較小的 OS 核心」。

2. **Precise trap 的定義**：**所有更早的指令都已退休，
   且沒有更晚的指令改動過架構狀態。**
   那個 `div` / `add` 的反例說明了 imprecise trap 為什麼無解——
   **回到哪裡都是錯的。**

3. **Precise trap 對微架構的要求**：**必須有一個 commit 級，
   把所有架構狀態的改動延遲到那裡。**
   **循序處理器用 Writeback 級就夠**（這解釋了 7.2 節那個設計原則），
   **亂序處理器需要 reorder buffer。**

還有一個歷史教訓：**Intel 1982 年就做了記憶體保護，
但軟體要到 2000 年的 Windows 2000 才用上——好硬體沒軟體配合等於沒有。**

下一節（8.2）進入 RISC-V 的具體做法。
