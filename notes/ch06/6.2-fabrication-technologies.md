## 關鍵點

### 製程節點：從 planar 到 FinFET

> "Integrated circuit fabrication technologies are characterized by their *process node* or
>  *feature size*. The 10-μm node through the 28-nm node, which were developed from 1972 until
>  2009, are known as *planar processes*, in which transistors are built from layers on a flat
>  silicon wafer.... **Processes typically scaled length and width by a factor of 1/√2 ≈ 0.7
>  from one node to the next, such that each node packed twice as many transistors in the same
>  area** [Dennard74]."
> — p.257

中文解釋：**縮放比例 0.7 的由來：0.7 × 0.7 ≈ 0.5，所以同面積放兩倍電晶體。**
（呼應 1.1 節的 Dennard scaling。）

**Dennard 縮放理論的兩個推論（p.258）：**
> "According to Dennard scaling theory, **delay scales proportionally to feature size and power
>  (per gate) scales quadratically with feature size.**"

**延遲與特徵尺寸成正比，每閘功耗與特徵尺寸成平方。**

**節點演進速度（p.258）：**
> "Vigorous worldwide competition led to new nodes every 2 to 3 years. Fig. 6.7 shows the
>  progression of Intel process nodes from 10 μm to 10 nm and beyond, indicating **a
>  thousand-fold feature size reduction and a million-fold increase in transistor density over
>  five decades or a doubling of transistor density every 2.5 years.**"

**五十年：特徵尺寸縮小 1000 倍，電晶體密度提高 100 萬倍，
相當於每 2.5 年密度翻倍。**
Fig. 6.7（p.258）是對數座標散佈圖，1970 到 2020 幾乎是一條直線。

### FinFET 與「節點名稱只是行銷」

> "As transistors shrank, the power supply voltage also scaled down to avoid damaging the tiny
>  devices. To maintain performance, the transistor threshold voltage also decreased.
>  **Unfortunately, the leakage of OFF transistors increases exponentially as threshold voltage
>  decreases. Below the 28-nm node, planar processes struggled to improve performance without
>  excessive leakage power. Chip manufacturers turned to FinFETs (fin-shaped field-effect
>  transistors), in which transistors are turned on their side and the gate is wrapped around
>  three sides to reduce leakage.**"
> — p.258

中文解釋：**完整的因果鏈（呼應 1.1 節）：**
```
電晶體縮小 → 供應電壓要降（避免損壞）
          → 臨界電壓也要降（維持效能）
          → 關閉時漏電呈指數上升
          → 28 nm 以下 planar 製程做不下去
          → 改用 FinFET（電晶體立起來，閘極包住三面以減少漏電）
```

> "**The process node is no longer linked to any physical dimension, but smaller nodes are now a
>  marketing term for general advances in materials and fabrication that improve performance or
>  power.** For example, TSMC describes that, compared to its 5-nm process, its 3-nm process has
>  33% better transistor density and is 10% to 15% faster at the same power or 25% to 30% lower
>  power at the same speed [Shilov20]."
> — p.258

中文解釋：**呼應 1.1 節：節點名稱只是行銷術語。**
TSMC 的 5nm → 3nm 實際改進：**密度 +33%、同功耗下快 10–15%、同速度下省電 25–30%。**
**這才是有意義的比較方式。**

### FO4：跨製程的通用時間單位

> "An FO4 inverter is an inverter driving four identical copies of itself. The FO4 delay is the
>  delay of such an inverter and **a handy metric for the overall performance of a process.
>  The theory of logical effort [Sutherland99] indicates that gates in well-designed fast
>  circuits have delays comparable to an FO4 inverter.**"
> — p.260

中文解釋：**FO4 = 一個反相器驅動四個相同反相器的延遲。**
（呼應 2.8.6 節：TSMC28 的 1 FO4 = 11.6 ps，Wally 各組態的週期是 29–108 FO4。）

**為什麼是「4」：logical effort 理論指出，
設計良好的快速電路裡，閘的延遲和 FO4 反相器相當。**
**所以 FO4 是「一個典型閘延遲」的代表值。**

### PVT corner：製程、電壓、溫度變異

> "All results in this book are reported under typical process, voltage, and temperature (PVT)
>  conditions defined earlier. Real circuits must operate over a range of temperatures; for
>  example, **commercial-grade parts are tested in an environment from 0° to 70°C, while
>  military-grade parts are tested from −55° to 125°C.** The transistor junction temperatures
>  can be substantially higher when the chip is drawing a lot of power. **The supply voltage
>  often varies by 10% or more because of voltage regulator error and power supply distribution
>  noise. Random fluctuations in the manufacturing process produce transistors that may be
>  faster or slower than typical (TT). Fast (FF) transistors have lower threshold voltages and
>  hence more leakage, while slow (SS) transistors have the opposite."
> — p.260

中文解釋：**PVT 三個變異來源，以及它們的範圍：**

| 變異 | 範圍 |
| --- | --- |
| **Process（製程）** | **TT（典型）、FF（快，低 V_t、多漏電）、SS（慢，高 V_t、少漏電）** |
| **Voltage（電壓）** | **常變動 ±10% 以上**（穩壓器誤差、電源分佈雜訊） |
| **Temperature（溫度）** | 商規 0–70°C，**軍規 −55 到 125°C** |

**注意 FF 和 SS 的取捨：快的電晶體漏電多，慢的漏電少。**
（呼應 6.1 節的 low-V_t 元件。）

**Table 6.2（p.261）的結論（p.260）：**
> "Table 6.2 summarizes INVX1 delay and leakage across these process, voltage, and temperature
>  corners, showing that **leakage increases by two orders of magnitude from typical to hot and
>  fast. Delay varies by roughly a factor of two between fast and slow extremes.**
>  **Products must leave *margin* so that they achieve specifications despite these variations.**"

**兩個要記住的量級：**
- **漏電：從典型到「熱 + 快」變化兩個數量級（100 倍）**
- **延遲：快慢兩極之間約差 2 倍**

**幾個 Table 6.2 的具體數字（TSMC28）：**
- TT / 標稱 / 25°C：FO4 = 11.6 ps，漏電 1.1 nW
- TT / 標稱 / 85°C：FO4 = 11.6 ps，**漏電 12.2 nW（×11）**
- SS / 標稱 / 125°C：FO4 = 12.8 ps，漏電 23.0 nW
- **FF / 標稱 / 125°C：FO4 = 10.8 ps，漏電 80.5 nW（×73）**
- **FF / +10% / 125°C：FO4 = 9.4 ps，漏電 111.8 nW（×100）**

**「快 + 熱 + 高壓」是漏電的最壞情況，「慢 + 冷 + 低壓」是延遲的最壞情況——
所以要同時檢查多個 corner。**

（Wally 的 `bin/libppa.pl` 腳本負責解析 Liberty 檔案，
萃取延遲、漏電、面積和電容，p.260 邊欄。）

### 資料通路元件的 PPA 縮放規律

> "Designers typically estimate the delay, area, and energy of functional units and memories when
>  planning a design. Moreover, **because fabrication technologies are constantly advancing, it is
>  useful to express delay and area in units that are independent of technology so that insights
>  from one design remain relevant in the next design. A typical metric is to list all delays
>  relative to an FO4 inverter.**"
> — p.260

中文解釋：**這是 FO4 的實際用途：讓一個設計的經驗能沿用到下一代製程。**

**"Functional unit delay tends to track FO4 inverter delay remarkably well, although paths
dominated by long wires do not."**（p.260）
——**功能單元延遲和 FO4 高度相關，但長導線主導的路徑不是。**

### 怎麼量測：掃描頻率

中文解釋：Fig. 6.8（p.262）是 SKY90 上 32-bit 加法器的實驗（p.261）：
> "To characterize the delay, area, power, and energy for each block, **many synthesis runs are
>  performed, sweeping the target frequency around the maximum achievable.** As target frequency
>  increases, the achieved delay initially decreases, and the area increases (gray dots).
>  **Beyond some target frequency, the delay ceases improving significantly, and the area and
>  delay become noisy (blue dots) as Design Compiler struggles to achieve an impossible target.
>  Selecting the rightmost gray dot gives a reasonably stable design point to measure the best
>  achievable delay and the resulting area and energy given that delay.**"

**這個方法論很值得學：**
1. **掃描目標頻率**（不是設一個就好）
2. 目標越高 → 延遲下降、面積上升（**灰點 = 達成時序**）
3. **超過某點後延遲不再改善，面積和延遲變得雜亂（藍點 = slack 違反）**
4. **取最右邊的灰點** = 穩定的最佳設計點

**從圖上讀：加法器在約 2400 MHz 前是灰點（延遲從 0.65 降到 0.42 ns，
面積從 800 升到 1250 μm²），之後就是雜亂的藍點。**

### Table 6.3：32-bit 快速加法器的三製程對照

中文解釋：**Table 6.3（p.262），TT 25°C：**

| 特性 | 單位 | SKY130 | SKY90 | TSMC28 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **延遲** | ps | 891 | 449 | **100** |
| | **FO4** | **9.0** | **10.4** | **8.6** |
| **面積** | μm² | 2,581 | 1,441 | **209** |
| 漏電功耗 | nW | 18 | 714 | 1,060 |
| 動態能量 | fJ/op | 685 | 658 | **82** |

**最重要的觀察：用 ps 表示時三個製程差很多（891 vs 100），
但用 FO4 表示時都在 8.6–10.4 之間——幾乎一樣。**

**這完美示範了 FO4 的價值：它把製程因素除掉了。
「32-bit 加法器大約是 9 個 FO4」是一個跨製程通用的知識。**

**量測條件（p.262）：**
- **動態能量用 clocked net 的活動因子 1、其他所有 net 用 0.1 量測**
  （**加法器是組合邏輯，所以沒有 clocked net**）
- 動態能量 = 動態功耗 × 時脈週期
- **漏電用靜態機率 0.5 量測**（每個 net 是 0 或 1 的機率相同）
- 這些是 Design Compiler 的預設值：
  `power_default_toggle_rate` 和 `power_default_static_probability`

**書上自己提出的質疑（p.262 邊欄）很值得注意：**
> "**It is suspicious that SKY130 and SKY90 have similar dynamic energies** (see Table 6.3)
>  because energy is usually lower at a smaller feature size. **It raises questions about whether
>  the libraries are characterized accurately.**"

**「較小的製程通常能量較低，這兩個卻差不多——
讓人懷疑元件庫的特徵化是否準確。」
——對自己的數據保持懷疑，這是好的工程態度。**

### Fig. 6.9 與 Table 6.4：元件 PPA 的縮放公式

中文解釋：Fig. 6.9（p.263–264）為 10 種元件各畫四張圖（延遲、面積、漏電、能量 vs 位元寬度），
每張含 90nm 和 28nm 的合成結果與平均曲線。

**讀圖規則（p.265）：**
- **x 軸是大小（8–128 bits）**
- 延遲面板：**最佳可達延遲，以 FO4 表示**
- **面積面板：實線 = 最佳延遲時的面積；虛線 = 不在乎延遲時的最小面積**
  > "For example, **an adder uses a prefix structure to achieve best delay, but it uses a much
  >  more compact ripple structure if delay doesn't matter.**"
- R² 擬合值大多接近 1，表示平均曲線對 90nm 和 28nm 都吻合得好

**Table 6.4（p.265）是本節最實用的表**——
**面積、漏電、能量都以「快速 add32」為單位，S = N/32 是正規化大小：**

| 元件 | **最佳延遲（FO4）** | 最佳延遲時 面積 / 漏電 / 能量 | 最小面積時 面積 / 漏電 / 能量 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `add` | **1.9 + 1.4 log₂N** | 1.0S / 0.89S / 0.91S | **0.32S** / 0.15S / 0.33S |
| `comparator` | 2.1 + 0.91 log₂N | **0.58S** / 0.44S / 0.27S | 0.33S / 0.15S / 0.12S |
| `priorityencoder` | 0.0 + 0.98 log₂N | 0.32S / 0.22S / 0.087S | 0.14S / 0.044S / 0.033S |
| `shiftleft` | 0.46 + 1.60 log₂N | **1.9S** / 2.1S / 1.4S | 0.77S / 0.28S / 0.48S |
| **`mult`** | **0.0 + 6.0 log₂N** | **13S²** / 13S² / 33S² | 7.8S² / 3.5S² / **14S²** |
| `mux2` | 2.6 + 0.41 log₂N | 0.21S / 0.23S / 0.16S | 0.14S / 0.11S / 0.14S |
| `mux4` | 3.1 + 0.50 log₂N | 0.35S / 0.31S / 0.26S | 0.27S / 0.11S / 0.19S |
| `mux8` | 4.9 + 0.46 log₂N | 0.80S / 0.66S / 0.43S | 0.53S / 0.23S / 0.24S |
| **`csa`** | **3.6**（常數！） | 0.87S / 1.3S / 0.95S | 0.33S / 0.15S / 0.31S |
| **`flop`** | **3.3**（常數！） | 0.33S / 0.33S / 1.3S | 0.33S / 0.33S / 1.3S |

**四個關鍵規律（p.265–266），這是架構估算的基礎：**

1. **多數元件延遲隨寬度的 log 成長**（`a + b·log₂N`）
2. **CSA（進位保留加法器）和 flop 的延遲是常數 3–4 FO4，與輸入數無關**
   > "flip-flops and carry-save adders have a delay of 3-4 FO4 independent of the number of
   >  inputs. **Microarchitects make early critical path estimates from these component delays.**"
3. **面積隨寬度線性成長**（**「連移位器也是，這有點意外」**），
   **但乘法器是二次方（S²）**
4. **早期估算的經驗值**：
   > "for example, **a comparator is about half the size of a fast adder, and a shifter is about
   >  twice the size.**"

**多工器延遲的特殊之處（p.266）：**
> "Note that multiplexer delay is measured from the select input and grows with the number of
>  inputs and bus width (N) **because the select signal must drive so many bits. The delay from
>  the data input is essentially independent of the number of bits.**"

**多工器的延遲要看從哪個輸入算起**——
從 select 算會隨寬度成長（因為 select 要驅動很多位元，**fanout 問題**），
**從資料輸入算則幾乎與位元數無關。**

### 記憶體：flop-based vs SRAM

> "On-chip RAM can be built from flip-flops or static RAM (SRAM) cells. **Flip-flops are easy to
>  use and are readily available with standard logic synthesis, but their area and power
>  consumption are far greater than those of SRAM. For example, in TSMC28, the flip-flop cell
>  area is 1.89 μm², while the SRAM bit cell area is 0.13 μm².**"
> — p.266

中文解釋：**單一 bit cell 的面積差距：1.89 vs 0.13 μm²，約 14.5 倍。**
（呼應 4.2.3.3 節：SRAM 比 flop-based 密約 10 倍。）

**但 SRAM 不是免費的（p.266）：**
> "However, SRAM requires additional *peripheral circuitry*, including a precharge circuit for
>  bitlines, sense amplifiers, and pitch-matched decoders. **It is created with SRAM generators,
>  provided by the fab or third-party IP vendors.**"

**ROM 的狀況（p.266）：**
> "**We find that the TSMC28 ROM generators have so much overhead that logic synthesis normally
>  produces smaller and faster results for a 128 × 32-bit boot ROM.**"

**小型 ROM 用邏輯合成反而比 ROM generator 好**——週期電路的開銷太大。

**取得 SRAM generator 的困難（p.266 邊欄）很值得知道：**
> "Obtaining SRAM generators can be difficult, especially for academic designers. **No Skywater
>  generator is available. The TSMC generator entails various licensing hassles, and it produces
>  a high aspect ratio with large column circuits and poor area utilization, so the area
>  advantage is less than expected.** At the time of this writing, **OpenRAM did not produce
>  satisfactory RAM decoders in our processes of interest.**"

**另一個替代方案（p.266 邊欄）：**
> "**If a multiport RAM is unavailable, one can synthesize a register file from latches rather
>  than flip-flops to save area.** For example, the RISC-V Ibex core optionally uses latches in
>  the register file. **They do not play nicely with FPGAs or Verilator**, which are optimized
>  for flop-based designs."

### Wally 怎麼處理記憶體

> "Wally by default describes memories with always_ff statements as in Section 4.2.3.3.2.
>  **When the USE_SRAM configuration is set, memories of specific sizes from Table 6.5 are
>  replaced with a memory wrapper module instantiation. The wrapper in turn should instantiate a
>  compiled SRAM macro for the target process. The names of these cells may be considered
>  confidential by the vendor, so they are not specified in this textbook nor the code.**"
> — p.266

中文解釋：**兩層抽象：Wally 的通用記憶體描述 → wrapper 模組 → 廠商的 SRAM macro。
廠商的元件名稱是機密，所以書上和程式碼都不給。**

**同步讀是共通點（p.266–267）：**
> "**SRAM modules are inherently synchronous with a read time involving an address setup t_setup
>  before the clock edge and a clock-to-Q delay t_cq to the read data output. Wally uses memories
>  with synchronous read, as given in Code Example 4.22, allowing either type of memory to be used
>  in the same pipeline.**"

**Wally 一律用同步讀，這樣 flop-based 和 SRAM 可以互換。**
（呼應 4.2.3.3 節：本書從 7.2.1 節起用同步記憶體。）

### Table 6.5：記憶體特性（很實用的對照）

中文解釋：**Table 6.5（p.267）的四種 Wally 記憶體。摘出 TSMC28 的 flop vs SRAM 對照：**

| 記憶體 | 用途 | 製程/樣式 | 面積(μm²) | t_setup(ps) | t_cq(ps) | 漏電(μW) | 動態能量(pJ/access) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ram1p1rwbe_64x128** | **Cache Data** | TSMC28 **Flop** | **33k** | 13 | **212** | **162** | **26** |
| | | TSMC28 **SRAM** | **7k** | 107 | 365 | **15** | **9** |
| **ram1p1rwbe_64x44** | Cache Tags | TSMC28 Flop | 12k | 13 | 192 | 58 | 8 |
| | | TSMC28 SRAM | **3k** | 107 | 305 | 6 | 4 |
| ram2p1r1wbe_64x32 | 分支歷史表 | TSMC28 Flop | 9k | 13 | 187 | 43 | 6 |
| | | TSMC28 SRAM | 8k | 50 | 459 | 20 | 4 |
| **ram2p1r1wbe_1024x68** | **分支目標緩衝區** | TSMC28 Flop | **261k** | 13 | 380 | **1,390** | 217 |
| | | TSMC28 SRAM | **48k** | 72 | 550 | **84** | **10** |

**四個關鍵觀察（對應 p.266 的說明）：**

1. **SRAM 面積小很多**：64×128 是 33k → 7k（**4.7 倍**），
   **1024×68 是 261k → 48k（5.4 倍）**
2. **SRAM 功耗低很多**：BTB 的漏電 1,390 → 84 μW（**16 倍**），
   動態能量 217 → 10 pJ（**22 倍**）
3. **但 flop-based 比較快**：t_cq 212 vs 365 ps，**而且 t_setup 13 vs 107 ps**
4. **陣列越大，SRAM 的優勢越明顯**
   > "**SRAM has greatest density and power benefits for large arrays where the peripheral area is
   >  modest compared to the bit cell array.**"
   **小陣列的週期電路開銷佔比太高**（呼應 4.2.3.3 節）。

**注意 t_setup 的巨大差距（13 vs 107 ps）**——
SRAM 要求位址提前很久就穩定，**這會吃掉前一級的時間預算。**

### 這些數字怎麼來的（方法論）

**SRAM 的數字（p.267）來自記憶體編譯器的 datasheet 目錄：**
- 面積來自 Area 項
- **t_cq 是 access time（t_ca）項**
- **t_setup 是 address setup（t_as）項**
- **漏電 = 總漏電流（μA）× 0.9 V**
- **動態能量 = 動態讀取電流（μA/MHz）× V_DD × 時脈週期**

**書上的實例計算（p.267）：**
> "For example, the 64 × 128-bit RAM has leakage current of 16.77 μA and thus leakage power of
>  16.77 μA × 0.9 V = 15.1 μW. It has dynamic read current of 11.5 μA/MHz and thus dynamic energy
>  of 11.5 μA/MHz × 0.9 V × 10⁻⁶ s = 10.35 pJ/read."

**單位換算的小陷阱（p.267 邊欄）值得看：**
> "Dynamic energy per read is actually multiplied by the read frequency (in MHz), but that cancels
>  with the read period (in μs). For example, **if a read occurs at a frequency of 2 MHz, the read
>  period is 1/2 μs. So, the energy per read is: 11.5 μA/MHz × 2 MHz/read × (0.9 V) × (1/2 μs),
>  which is still 10.35 pJ/read.**"

**頻率和週期互相抵消，所以每次讀取的能量與頻率無關。**

**flop-based 的數字（p.268）來自 Design Compiler 合成報告：**
```bash
$ make synth DESIGN=ram1p1rwbe TECH=sky90 CONFIG=syn_rv64gc FREQ=10000 USESRAM=0
```
> "**The design is synthesized with an impossibly fast target (10 GHz) to estimate the best speed.**
>  The area and leakage numbers come straight from synthesis reports. The dynamic energy is the
>  reported dynamic power (in mW) multiplied by the 100-ps target period. The t_cq read delay
>  comes from the timing report. **t_setup is obtained as the setup time of a flip-flop from another
>  synthesis run such as wallypipelinedcore.**"

**「用一個不可能達成的目標頻率（10 GHz）來估最佳速度」——這是個實用的技巧。**

## 所以呢
