# 4.1 HDL Design Methodology

> 原書 p.141–145（Chapter 4: HDL Design Practices）
> 含章首導論、4.1.1 Design Strategies、4.1.2 Version Control、4.1.3 Reuse

## 這節在講什麼

還沒開始寫程式碼，先講**怎麼管理複雜度**。

章首導論先比較各種 HDL（為什麼選 SystemVerilog），然後三個子節是三種控制複雜度的手段：
1. **設計策略**（4.1.1）：規格書 → 方塊圖 → HDL。**最重要的一句是「不要把 HDL 當程式寫」。**
2. **版本控制**（4.1.2）
3. **重用**（4.1.3）：買現成 IP，自己只做「特調醬料」

**本節最該帶走的是那個 beginner mistake 警告，以及「先想硬體、再寫程式碼」的順序。**

## 關鍵點

### 為什麼 HDL 比電路圖好：抽象層次

> "An advantage of HDLs over schematics is that the logic can be specified at a higher
>  level of abstraction called *behavioral* or *register transfer level* (RTL). An RTL
>  description focuses on the registers and the datapath elements, such as adders,
>  shifters, and multiplexers, rather than individual logic gates or transistors."
> — p.141

> "Moreover, the logic synthesis tool can automatically pick the most appropriate
>  implementation for given constraints. For example, adders can be built using ripple
>  carry, carry lookahead, or prefix tree architectures, with increasing speed but also
>  increasing cost. Given timing constraints, a logic synthesis tool will try to pick the
>  lowest-cost implementation that meets timing."
> — p.141

中文解釋：**RTL（暫存器轉移層級）的重點是「關注暫存器和資料通路元件，而非個別閘」。**

**第二段是 HDL 真正的威力：你寫 `+`，合成工具幫你選加法器架構。**
ripple carry（慢但便宜）→ carry lookahead → prefix tree（快但貴），
**工具會在滿足時序的前提下挑最便宜的那個。**

**這就是為什麼抬高抽象層次能提升生產力**（呼應 1.2 節「每次生產力危機的解法都是往上抬一層」）。

### 為什麼選 SystemVerilog

> "The Wally processor uses SystemVerilog [IEEE1800-2023], a modern flavor of Verilog.
>  Verilog is the most widely used HDL in the industry. SystemVerilog fixes some
>  syntactical annoyances of Verilog (e.g., the reg data type, which might or might not
>  be a register) and adds powerful capabilities for verification that are beyond the
>  scope of this book. SystemVerilog is nearly interchangeable with Verilog and is well
>  supported in modern tools, so there is little reason to write new code in classic
>  Verilog anymore."
> — p.141–142

中文解釋：**選 SystemVerilog 的三個理由：業界最廣用、修掉 Verilog 的毛病、工具支援好。**

**`reg` 那個吐槽很經典**：Verilog 的 `reg` **不一定是暫存器**——
名字騙人，害無數初學者搞錯。SystemVerilog 用 `logic` 取代它。

**重要警告（p.142）：**
> "SystemVerilog has many nonsynthesizable constructs for writing testbenches, and the
>  SystemVerilog standard is not explicit about what is synthesizable or not."

**語言標準本身沒有明確定義「哪些語法能合成」。**
所以本書強調 **standard idioms（標準慣用寫法）**——
**照慣用寫法寫，才知道會合成出什麼。**

（書上說本書會把 Verilog 和 SystemVerilog 兩個名字混用。）

### 其他 HDL 的處境

> "The leading competitor to Verilog is VHDL [IEEE1076-2019], which once was dominant in
>  Europe and at US military contractors but is steadily declining in market share
>  [Golson16]. The synthesizable subset of VHDL is essentially interchangeable with
>  SystemVerilog, but idiosyncrasies of the language lead to about 50% more code to
>  describe the same hardware."
> — p.142

中文解釋：**各家 HDL 的定位整理：**

| 語言 | 狀況 |
| --- | --- |
| **SystemVerilog** | **業界主流** |
| **VHDL** | 曾在歐洲和美國軍方承包商佔優勢，**市佔持續下滑**。可合成子集功能等價，但**同樣的硬體要多寫約 50% 程式碼** |
| **SystemC** | C++ class library，抽象層次更高，對熟練使用者能縮短設計時間 |
| **Chisel、SpinalHDL、Bluespec** | 宣稱生產力更高，但**還沒取得大市佔** |

（呼應 2.9 節 Table 2.12：Berkeley BOOM 用 Chisel3、Madras Shakti 用 BlueSpec、
VexRiscv 用 SpinalHDL——**新語言主要出現在學術界開源核心**。）

「主流的邏輯模擬與合成工具三種語言都支援」（p.142）。

### 設計策略：規格書的真實地位

> "Sophisticated design teams write specifications before writing code. The
>  specifications define the system functions and design hierarchy. A good specification
>  helps all members of a team understand how the system functions and where different
>  features are implemented. Getting the hierarchy right the first time reduces effort
>  repartitioning the system later."
> — p.142

> "However, the ultimate goal of the project is working code, not polished documentation,
>  so managers must remember that the specifications are merely a means toward an end."
> — p.142

中文解釋：**兩段要一起讀，因為作者很平衡：**
- **正面**：規格書定義系統功能與**設計階層**；一次把階層切對，可以省下後來重新切分的功夫
- **反面**：**專案的最終目標是能動的程式碼，不是漂亮的文件**

書上也承認現實（p.142–143）：
- **經驗不足的設計者沒有參考框架，往往得先寫點程式碼才懂系統，之後再補文件**
- **新創公司常常省略規格書**，急著在資金燒完前做出最小可行產品，
  **賭的是一小群設計者把設計記在腦子裡，希望沒人在產品完成前被卡車撞到**

（最後那句是書上的原話，"hoping that none of the designers get hit by a truck
before the product is ready"。）

### 從方塊圖開始，由上而下

> "After the requirements are defined, start by thinking of the design with block
>  diagrams. Begin with a top-level block diagram and draw the inputs and outputs. Early
>  in the design phase, it may be most practical to focus on the major input and output
>  busses and leave their associated control signals for later. Then, sketch the
>  sub-blocks and how they connect to the primary inputs and outputs and to internal
>  signals between sub-blocks. Repeat this process hierarchically for each sub-block
>  until it is simple enough to understand."
> — p.143

中文解釋：**具體的步驟，很實用：**
1. 先畫**頂層方塊圖**和輸入輸出
2. **先只關注主要的資料匯流排，控制訊號留到後面**
   ← **這個順序很重要**：先把資料流搞對，控制訊號自然浮現
   （呼應 2.7 節：資料通路固定，控制訊號只是指揮多工器）
3. 往下畫子方塊，**遞迴進行直到簡單到能理解為止**

**"Often, different blocks are owned by different designers and communicate via
interfaces specified early in the design process."**（p.143）
——**介面要早點定下來**，因為不同人負責不同方塊。

### 最常見的新手錯誤（本節最重要的一句）

> "After you get down to simple blocks, design them in terms of hardware components such
>  as registers, multiplexers, ALUs, memories, and combinational logic blocks.
>  **The most common beginner mistake is to write HDL like a program, and the more
>  programming experience one has, the easier it is to make this mistake.**"
> — p.143

中文解釋：**這句話值得貼在牆上。**

**而且它有個反直覺的推論：程式設計經驗越豐富，越容易犯這個錯。**

理由不難理解：HDL 的語法長得像程式語言（有 `if`、`case`、`for`），
但**它描述的是同時存在的硬體，不是依序執行的指令**。
寫程式的直覺（「這行先跑，那行後跑」）在這裡是錯的。

**正確的順序是**（p.143，以及 4.2 節開頭）：
**先在腦中看見想要的硬體（暫存器、多工器、ALU、記憶體），再寫出隱含該硬體的 HDL 慣用寫法。**

書上給的操作方法（p.143）：
> "To obtain good power, performance, and area, and to minimize design errors, designers
>  use only a *synthesizable subset* of SystemVerilog where they can readily visualize
>  the hardware implied by their code. For each hardware component, write the
>  corresponding idiom from Section 4.2. **If you aren't sure what hardware you intend to
>  imply with some code, you are unlikely to get good results from that code.**"

**「如果你不確定某段程式碼想產生什麼硬體，那段程式碼大概不會有好結果。」**

### Waterfall vs Agile

> "*Waterfall* project management is a traditional approach drawn from mature industries
>  such as construction. The project is divided into tasks, such as conception,
>  initiation, design, building, testing, and deployment. Each step is reviewed and then
>  frozen before moving on to the next step. It works well when the time and cost of each
>  step can be predicted well from experience and when risk is low."
> — p.143

> "Because testing of the overall design does not occur until much later in the project
>  when using this method, major flaws may not be revealed until late in the process when
>  they are most expensive to fix, and sometimes the problems in the underlying design are
>  insurmountable so late in the process [Keating02]. In contrast, *agile* project
>  management is an iterative design process that emphasizes delivering frequent working
>  prototypes, getting rapid customer feedback, and embracing changing requirements."
> — p.143

中文解釋：**兩種方法的適用條件很清楚：**

| | Waterfall（瀑布式） | Agile（敏捷式） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 做法 | 分階段，**每階段審查後凍結**才進下一步 | **迭代**，頻繁交付可運作的原型 |
| 適合 | 每階段時間成本可準確預估、**風險低** | 需求會變、風險高 |
| 致命傷 | **整體測試太晚才發生**，重大缺陷發現時已經最貴、甚至無法補救 | — |

歷史細節：軟體專案歷史上是瀑布式的——
**美國國防部 1985 年的 DOD-STD-2167A 標準明文要求國防合約採用此法**（p.143）。

**結論（p.144）：**
> "Except when an SoC is a minor update to an existing product, agile methods tend to be
>  most appropriate for SoC design. **Wally was developed using an agile methodology.**"

**除非是小改版，SoC 設計適合敏捷。Wally 就是敏捷開發的。**
（呼應 2.10 節「每個功能都從 test plan 開始」——先想怎麼測，就是敏捷的「頻繁驗證」。）

### 版本控制

> "*Version control* is the practice of tracking and managing changes to code. Version
>  control systems maintain a database, called a *repository*, of all of the code files,
>  tracking every modification. The main branch of the code is intended to be stable and
>  robust. Developers create other branches as they write and test new features, then
>  merge them into the main branch when ready."
> — p.144

> "If a bug is introduced, version control makes it easy to roll back to the last working
>  version of the system and to identify exactly what changes have been made since that
>  working version."
> — p.144

中文解釋：**版控在硬體設計的價值和軟體一樣**，兩個重點：
1. **main branch 保持穩定**，新功能在別的 branch 開發，好了再 merge
2. **出 bug 時能回退，而且能精確找出「從能動的版本到現在改了什麼」**

**開源治理**（p.144）：
"Open-source development is driven by public repositories managed by a team of
*committers* who review and accept contributions."
——**committer（提交者）負責審查和接受貢獻。**

**Wally 用 Git**（Appendix C），**GitHub 當雲端託管**。

**即使一個人做小專案也值得用**（p.144）：
檔案備份、容易回到上一個能動的設計、可以儲存與分享工作。
**"When developers get accustomed to version control, they rarely go back."**

邊欄的職涯建議（p.144 邊欄）：
> "A student with a rich set of code in their public GitHub repositories and a history of
>  making contributions to other projects has a substantial edge in an interview!"

### 重用：最有效的成本控制手段

> "The most effective strategy for controlling design cost is to reuse proven components.
>  The SoC design team typically licenses cell, memory, and input/output (I/O) libraries
>  from the chip foundry or reuses libraries developed internally. The team then adds
>  components such as processor cores, peripherals, and accelerators."
> — p.144

中文解釋：呼應 1.2 節的 design reuse。**SoC 的組裝清單分三層：**

| 層級 | 內容 | 來源 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **底層** | cell、memory、I/O 函式庫 | **向晶圓代工廠授權**，或內部既有的 |
| **中層** | 處理器核心、周邊、加速器 | 第三方授權或自家專有函式庫 |
| **頂層** | **"special sauce" 客製化方塊** | **自己做的差異化部分** |

**具體的市場數字**（p.144）：
- **2023 年 SoC 出貨超過 300 億顆 ARM 核心**
- **到 2022 年估計出貨 100 億顆 RISC-V 核心**，且**呈指數成長**
- **核心複雜度從數萬到數千萬顆電晶體，效能跨兩個數量級**

**具體的元件清單**（p.144–145）：
- **周邊**：DDR 與 Flash 記憶體控制器、Wi-Fi/藍牙/行動通訊的無線電、
  有線乙太網路與序列連結、計時器、ADC/DAC
- **加速器**：GPU、視訊編解碼器、AI、密碼學、網路專用方塊

**加速器的效益再次量化**（p.145，呼應 1.2 節）：
> "Accelerators operate in parallel with processors and typically offer two orders of
>  magnitude better performance and energy efficiency than general-purpose processors
>  programmed for the same task."

**兩個數量級（100 倍）的效能和能效優勢。**

**設計團隊真正在做的事**（p.145）：
> "The design team may add some "special sauce" blocks customized to the target
>  application and then integrates all the parts onto an SoC, verifies the system, and
>  develops software to run on the chip."

**加自家的特調方塊 → 整合 → 驗證 → 開發軟體。**
**注意「整合、驗證、軟體」佔了三項**——呼應 1.2 節 Fig. 1.5 的成本組成
（軟體最大、驗證第二）。

| 名詞 | 意思 |
| --- | --- |
| RTL | Register transfer level。關注暫存器與資料通路元件的抽象層次 |
| SystemVerilog | IEEE1800-2023。業界主流 HDL；修正 Verilog 的 `reg` 等問題並強化驗證能力 |
| VHDL | IEEE1076-2019。可合成子集等價，但同樣硬體要多寫約 50% 程式碼 |
| SystemC | IEEE1666-2011。C++ class library，抽象層次更高 |
| Synthesizable subset | 可合成子集。標準未明確定義，故仰賴慣用寫法 |
| Waterfall | 瀑布式。分階段凍結；整體測試太晚，缺陷發現時代價最高 |
| **Agile** | 敏捷式。迭代、頻繁交付可運作原型。**Wally 採用此法** |
| Repository / branch / merge | 版控的資料庫 / 分支 / 合併 |
| Committer | 開源專案中負責審查與接受貢獻的維護者 |
| Design reuse / IP | 重用已驗證的元件，是控制設計成本最有效的策略 |
| Special sauce | 設計團隊自己加的客製化差異方塊 |

## 所以呢

**本節最該記住的是那個順序：先想硬體，再寫 HDL——
「最常見的新手錯誤是把 HDL 當程式寫，而且程式經驗越多越容易犯」。**

實務三件事：
1. **從方塊圖開始，先畫資料匯流排、控制訊號留後面**，遞迴細分到能理解為止。
2. **SoC 適合敏捷，不適合瀑布**——因為瀑布的整體測試太晚，缺陷發現時已經無法補救。
3. **自己只做「special sauce」，其餘買現成的**——這是控制成本最有效的策略。
